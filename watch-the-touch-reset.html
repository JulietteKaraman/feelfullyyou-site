Questions you might have

Do I need a partner?

No. It begins with your relationship with your own body. If you have a partner, they don't need to join. There are practices you can choose to explore together, and their taking part is never required.

What if I have a history of trauma?

I have been working with trauma and the body for the last 10 years, and I experience it deeply myself. This is designed to meet you where you are. You will never be asked to push through a no, override your body's response or share anything you don't want to share. It's a group programme, and it doesn't replace individual therapy or medical care.

What if I'm nervous about being seen?

Most women are, at the start. Camera on or off, your choice, and you choose how much you share. The group goes gently, a little more each week.

How much time will it take?

About two hours a week at most, the live call included. Every call is recorded, so you can always catch up.

How much is it?

There are a few ways to pay, and we go through them on your Touch Reset Call, once we both know this is right for you.
