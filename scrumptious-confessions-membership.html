Why I built it

My DMs are full of the questions people never say out loud.

About touch. About desire. About the nights it goes quiet and nobody knows why.

Who I am

I'm Juliette.

Intimacy and touch expert. I teach The Touch Reset: finding the patterns that close the door to touch, aliveness and connection, and learning how to open it again.

I started as a SENCO teacher, helping autistic children feel safe in their own skin. Then fifteen years in the room with couples, and with trauma.

Before I was the one in that room, I was the woman on the other side of it. Stiffening when the man I loved reached for me. Pulling away, with no words for why.
