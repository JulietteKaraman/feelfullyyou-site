Why I built it

It started with an app for my women’s group.

They kept telling me the same thing.

“I want to hear your voice all the time.”

So I put my voice in there. Meditations. Practices. Poems. Then I noticed how much I already had…. Years of it, sitting in folders.

Then there are the DMs, every day.

“Can we hop on a call?”
“Can you help me with this?”

I can’t get on a call with everyone. I wish I could. What I can do is build one place that holds all the questions, and my answers.

So I did. Now women and men can ask me anything.

I'm Juliette.

Intimacy and Touch Expert. I teach The Touch Reset: finding the patterns that close the door to Touch, aliveness and connection, and learning how to open it again.

Every week, in my voice, in the app. Every answer goes into the library, so you can read the ones I’ve already given too.

“Do I need a partner?”
No. Single, in a couple, somewhere in between. It works for all of it.

Yes. Men ask me a lot, and I’ve answered a lot of their questions over the years.
