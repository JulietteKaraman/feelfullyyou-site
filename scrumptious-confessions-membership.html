FAQs

Will anyone know the question is mine?

No. Your question goes on the members’ wall with no name and no email on it. Not even I can see who sent it.

What can I ask?

Anything about Touch, desire, sex and your relationship. The questions you’d never post where people can see your name.

When are questions answered?

Every Thursday, in the app. Every answer goes into the library, so you can read the ones I’ve already given too.

What do I get in my free week?

A taste of everything: the confessions, the cards, the poems, Touch Base, every question on the wall, ten pages of the Journal and three meditations. Plus two gifts to keep, whatever you decide: the Holiday Survival Kit and Quiet Isn’t Yes. On day 8, after your first payment, the full library and The Trace open.

When am I charged?

Your card goes in to start. Nothing is charged during your 7 free days, then it’s £111 a month. Join before Monday 23 November and your free days start that Monday, when doors open, so your first payment is on Monday 30 November. Stop before your first payment and you pay nothing.

Will the price go up?

Not for you. Join now and your £111 never goes up. When the price goes up for new members, you still pay £111, every month you stay a member. If you stop and come back later, you’d join at the new price.

Do I need a partner?

No. Single, in a couple, somewhere in between. It works for all of it.

Is it really for men too?

Yes. Men ask me a lot, and I’ve answered a lot of their questions over the years. Those answers are all in there, with embodiment exercises, and two guides written for men, Quiet Isn’t Yes and The Unspoken Distance.

Is this therapy?

No. I’m a hypnotherapist, an EMDR therapist, a coach and a Touch expert, and I’ve worked with trauma for many years. I’m not a psychotherapist or a psychologist, and this app isn’t therapy. It’s my voice, my stories and what I teach. If something heavy comes up, please talk to someone you trust, or your GP. You don’t have to carry it on your own.

What if I can’t get in?

Tap “Can’t get in? Check my membership” in the app and it checks your payment on the spot. If a payment ever fails, you keep access for 7 days while your card is tried again. Still stuck? Email support@feelfullyyou.com and we’ll get you in.

How do I stop?

In the app, any time. Scroll to the bottom of any screen and tap “Manage or cancel my membership”. It takes a minute, with no forms and no questions. You keep everything until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and every programme that opened in a month you paid for stays yours for good. Stop in your first 7 days and you pay nothing.
