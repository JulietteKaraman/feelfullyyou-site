An evening with Juliette

Arrive. Receive. Feel. Keep.

I learned the order the hard way. Ask a room of strangers to feel something in a space with no edges, and some feel nothing at all. Settle the body first, and the same touch lands.

Arrive

Touch Base®. Thumb to forefinger, feel every ridge, "I am safe right now." Two minutes.

Receive

Spinal Attunement. 45 minutes lying down, fully clothed, in silence.

Feel

Slow touch on their own wrist. Now they can actually feel it.

Keep

31 days of cards on their phone, in my voice, and the method to go with them.

The heart of it

Spinal Attunement, for groups.

A nervous system reset. Completely hands-off. Everyone lies down and simply receives. Nobody has to talk, share or perform.

I trained in it at Soma Mystica, and I bring it together with my own trauma-informed frequency work.

  • A subtle wave moving through the spine
  • Emotion moving, without being stirred back up
  • Deep stillness, softening or clarity
  • An exhale they didn't know they were holding

What the evening is made of

Seven pieces. We pick what fits your room.

01 · Arrive

Touch Base®

The two-minute anchor I teach first in every room.

02 · Receive

Spinal Attunement

45 minutes. Hands-off, lying down, in silence.

03 · Feel

Touch on the wrist

A few slow minutes. After the attunement, people can actually feel it.

04 · Map

A first taste of the Touch Map™

Which touch they like, where, and how much. The full map is my one-to-one work.

05 · Ask

The Tea Experience

Asking for exactly what you want. Funny for a minute, then it isn't. For women's gatherings and retreats.

06 · Say it

A way of communicating

Built on my Intimacy and Communication Cards, so people say the thing they've been circling and get heard.

07 · Keep

31 days of cards

Every guest takes home 31 Days Closer, with my voice, and the method.
