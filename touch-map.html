How the map is made: A and B

The same place, touched two different ways, until the body shows you how it is wired. Touch A on the neck might be energetic, barely landing. Touch B on the neck might be firm and certain. You try both, then you write down which one that zone actually wanted.

Do not try the whole body in one sitting. Take one region a month. By the twelfth month you have been through the whole body once, properly, and you actually know your ❌ ✅ 🔶, not a vague sense of it. Redo the same region the following year before moving to the next.

How to fill it in

Start with yourself. Your own hand first, on your own skin, before anyone else has an opinion about it. You cannot tell someone where touch lands if you have never checked.

One zone, two touches. Touch A, then touch B, in the same place. Energetic then firm. Feather then whole palm. Then write down which one that zone wanted, in your own words, and not the word you think you should write.

Then swap maps. This is the part most couples have never done. Not a conversation about what you like. A map, in their handwriting, that you get to keep.

Redo it every month. Bodies change. A no in January is often a maybe by March, and the only way you find out is by asking again.
