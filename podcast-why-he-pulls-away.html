Why Does He Pull Away the Moment You Get Close?

When you reach and he steps back, it is almost never that he does not want you. Here is what his body is doing, and the read that stops the chase.

Juliette KaramanTouch and Intimacy Expert
A solo teaching from The Scrumptious Woman podcast

You reach for him. A hand on his shoulder while he is stacking the dishwasher. You lean in, you want a little more. You feel it happen. He goes still. Something in him steps back even though his feet do not move. Maybe he makes a joke, maybe he remembers something he has to do. You stand there with your hand cooling on his shoulder, wondering what you did. Wondering if it is you.

For years I read men wrong too, and the many couples who came to me. I thought the step back meant he did not want his partner. It almost never means that.

What Happens in the Half Second

You reached for connection. His body read it as a demand it might fail. Not a thought, a reflex, older than the two of you. Somewhere he learned that a woman reaching means a test is coming, that closeness arrives with a bill he is not sure he can pay right there and then. So the moment you get warm, his system braces and creates distance, because distance feels survivable and closeness feels like exposure. He still wants you. His body is bracing, the same half second yours does at the sink, pointed the other way.

Why Your Loving Reach Gets a Defensive Answer

This is the part that can change your week. When you say I miss you, or can we talk, a braced man's nervous system does not hear longing. It hears you are not enough, and for many men that is the deepest wound, the fear that they are not enough, not providing enough. A bid for closeness and a criticism land in the very same place in him. What he defends against is the feeling of failing you. That is the thing he is fighting, and it was never you.

When he goes quiet, that is the brace. His heart is still in the room. Pulling away is a stage two pattern, the same map as yours, his version of it. If you read the distance as proof that he has checked out, you chase, and chasing reads as another demand, so he braces harder. You have both run this loop for years, and neither of you is the villain in it.

The Read: Warm, Weight, Where

Before you reach, read him. Three points. Warm, is his body warm and loose, or cool and held. Weight, is he settled into the chair, into the floor, or perched and ready to move. Where, is his attention here in the room with you, or already on whatever he is doing. Ten seconds, and he will never know you did it. If he is cool, perched and gone, that is not the moment to reach for more. That is the moment to give him something with no ask inside it. Sit near him, say nothing, let his body feel a woman close who wants nothing from him for sixty seconds. Your nervous system will start to settle his. Closeness with no bill, the thing he has rarely had. And one prompt before any touch, ask him, are you present enough for me to touch you right now.

"The next time he steps back while his feet stay still, you will know what you are looking at. It is the brace. It was never a verdict on you. Being touched isn't the same as feeling touch, and patterns can be changed."
