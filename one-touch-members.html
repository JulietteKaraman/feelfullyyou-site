Intro Welcome to One Touch
the welcome

Hello, my loves, and welcome to One Touch. Over the next seven days we dig deeply, but softly, into the conditioning that may have kept you from touch that lands. So many of us live in our heads, disconnected from the body.

Each day is a short embodiment practice, and I'll teach you Touch Base, a simple anchor that quiets the fight, the flight and the fear, so your body can feel safe. It always brings people back to calm.

One thing: please don't skip ahead. These days are sequential, each one built on the one before, like a Lego tower. Without the foundation, you can't go higher. Start when you're ready.
