Frequently asked

Do we do the calls together?

Both. There are individual calls tailored to your role, and couple calls where you're held together.

Is this therapy?

The Beginning works with both the body and the psyche, because both are involved in why touch stops landing. I bring structure to all of it: the body, the emotions, the relational pattern. Using Touch Languages™, EMDR, spinal attunement, and Rapid Release Rewire Restore®, we work at the level where things actually shift.

Is it safe for us if one of us has trauma?

Yes. I've worked with trauma and the body for years, and I know it deeply in my own body too. Nothing is forced, and we go at the pace of your bodies. Saying no, or stop, is usually exactly what's hard, so it becomes part of the work: each of you gets to feel, in your own body, where your yes is and where your no is.

What if one of us isn't sure?

That's what the touch reset call is for. We talk, I listen, and we work out together whether this is the right fit. If it isn't, I will tell you. Thirty minutes, no pressure, completely confidential.

Can we do this if we live in different time zones?

Yes. The calls are scheduled individually, so different time zones are no barrier. The programme works online globally.

You have access to this material for a lifetime, and your bespoke audio is yours to keep. Many couples continue with bespoke mentorship or the Couples Intensive. The Beginning sets a foundation, not a ceiling.
