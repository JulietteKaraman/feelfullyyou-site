What this actually is

The Touch Reset is a 12-week practice in becoming deeply familiar with your own body.

Not so you can make yourself want more touch.

Not so you can push through a no.

So you can recognise what is actually happening before you automatically react to it.

Is this a real no?
Is something old being activated?
Am I bracing, performing or going quiet?
Does this actually feel good?
What do I want instead?

Then you practise what comes next.

Asking. Adjusting. Saying yes. Saying no. Asking for more. Asking for something different. Stopping.

Because the goal isn't to make your body respond the "right" way.

It's to know your body well enough that you can tell what's true for you, and have the tools to do something with that information.

Who this is for

Different situations. One place to begin: your own body.

You don't need to have the same story as somebody else in the group.
You don't need to be in a relationship.
You don't even need to know exactly what's wrong.

You may simply know that the way you're experiencing intimacy, touch or pleasure isn't the way you want to keep experiencing it.

You love each other, but somewhere along the way, touch changed.

Sex may have stopped altogether, or you're still having it but one or both of you is going through the motions.

You want to find your way back without making either of you the problem.

Your own desire has changed

Something that used to work doesn't anymore.

Maybe illness, children, stress, trauma or simply life has changed the way your body responds.

Perhaps you've quietly started wondering:

"Maybe this is just how I am now."

You don't seem to want the same things

Maybe you've called it mismatched libido.

But frequency might not be the whole story.

You may simply experience pleasure differently, different touch, different pacing, different energy, different ways of being approached.

Maybe you don't want less. Maybe you want different.

You're doing this without a partner

Maybe you're single, dating, between relationships or simply doing this for yourself.

You can still recognise the patterns: performing, accommodating, struggling to receive or going blank when somebody asks:

"What do you want?"

You don't need another person to start discovering the answer.

Different bodies. Different histories. Different desires. Different patterns.

The work is learning yours.

How the 12 weeks actually work

Recognise. Discern. Discover. Ask & receive.

We don't start by trying to make you more sexual or fix your relationship. We start by learning how to listen.

The Touch Reset gives you structure, around your time, your communication, your self-touch and your partnered touch, so you have somewhere to practise something different.

Over 12 weeks, we move through four stages.

1
Stage 1

Recognise

What is my body already doing?

We slow things down enough for you to notice your automatic responses around intimacy, communication and touch.

The bracing. The performing. The accommodating. The reaching. The pulling away.

Using practices including Touch Base, Dyads and the half-second gap, you begin catching those moments earlier.

First, we notice.

2
Stage 2

Discern

What is this response actually telling me?

Is this my truth? A genuine boundary? Fear? Activation? An old protective pattern?

We explore what's underneath your responses, including beliefs and shame around your body, sex, pleasure and what you're allowed to want.

Where appropriate, I also bring in tools from EMDR and hypnotherapy.

The aim isn't to endlessly excavate the past. It's to give you more choice in the present.

3
Stage 3

Discover

What actually feels good to me?

This is where we explore your Touch Languages, Pleasure Language and Touch Map.

You begin discovering what your body responds to, touch, sensation, pressure, pace, energy, anticipation.

What lights you up? What turns you off? What have you been tolerating because you didn't know there were other options?

It's difficult to ask somebody else for what you want when you don't yet know what that is.

4
Stage 4

Ask & Receive

Can I bring what I've discovered into intimacy?

Now you practise asking for what you want.

Adjusting. Receiving. Giving. Saying yes. Saying no. Changing your mind.

For those with a partner, structured touch dates give you somewhere to practise together. If you don't have a partner, the work still begins with you.

Sex can absolutely change as a result of this work. But sex isn't where we start.

We start with recognising.

Then discerning.

Then discovering.

Then asking and receiving.

That's the reset.

Questions you might have.

How much time do I need each week?

Around two hours a week at most, including the live session and practices. The practices are small and designed to fit into real life.

Do I need a partner?

No. The Touch Reset begins with your relationship with your own body. If you do have a partner, they don't need to join the programme with you. There are practices you can choose to explore together, but their participation isn't required.

What if I have a history of trauma?

Juliette has worked extensively with trauma for the last 15 years and is EMDR trained. The Touch Reset is designed to meet you where you are. You will never be asked to push through a no, override your body's response or share anything you don't want to share. You choose your pace, your boundaries and what you participate in.

The Touch Reset is a group programme and is not a replacement for individual therapy or medical care.

Will I have to talk about my sex life in front of everyone?

No. You choose what you share. The group is there to practise and learn together, not to require you to disclose intimate details. You don't have to expose yourself to belong in the room.

What if I miss a live call?

The sessions are recorded so you can catch up. Come live whenever you can. The value is in practising together, asking questions and learning from what happens in the room.

My commitment to you.

The Touch Reset is something we practise together.

Your part is to show up, engage with the work and bring the practices into your life.

My part is to give you the structure, tools and support to do that.

If you've participated in at least 80% of the programme, genuinely engaged with the practices, and at the end of the 12 weeks you feel you need more support integrating what you've learned, I'll give you an additional 30 days of group support at no extra cost.
