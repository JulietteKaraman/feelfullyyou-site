The Sacred No.

Seven lessons, a few minutes each. Watch them in any order, at any pace, there is no wrong way to move through this.

Before you begin
Welcome How often do you say yes and mean no?

The challenge ahead of you: seven short lessons to start noticing what happens in your body in the exact moment a yes and a no are both trying to speak at once.

Lesson 1 Why is no so difficult?

Where this actually comes from: how we're conditioned from childhood to keep pushing until a no becomes a yes, and why that pattern followed you into every relationship since.

Lesson 2 Use your body as a compass

A simple kinesiology practice to feel your own body's real yes and real no, so you can recognise the difference before your mind talks you out of it.

Lesson 3 The Rapid Release Rewire Restore® process

A full process for working through the emotion still sitting underneath a time you said yes when your body was already saying no.

Lesson 4 Touch Base

Two minutes, your own hands. The anchor that brings your body back to safe enough to actually feel what it wants, before you answer anyone.

Lesson 5 Standards with others

Where your boundaries are leaking with the people around you, friends, family, the ones who drain more than they give. A real look at your nos and your yeses with other people.

Lesson 6 Standards with yourself

The standards you keep once no one else is watching, phones at night, how you talk to yourself, what your own routine says about what you believe you deserve.

Lesson 7 The last day

A closing reflection and a release practice, on everything you've noticed this week and what's already started to shift.

Bonus A guided meditation

A full guided meditation on saying no as an act of love for yourself. Come back to this any time you need it, long past the seven lessons.

What's next
