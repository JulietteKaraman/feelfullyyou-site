If You Feel Everything in a Room, This Is for You

Energetic sensitivity is not too much. It is a form of perception most of us had as children, before the world taught us to override it.

Juliette KaramanTouch and Intimacy Expert
A solo teaching from The Scrumptious Woman podcast

Everything is matter or energy, and the two are more connected than most of us were raised to believe. Every thought pattern, every movement, leaves an imprint. If you have ever walked into a room and known, before anyone said a word, that something was off, you were not imagining it. You were reading energy.

Children Already Know This

Watch a young child in a new house. They will often gravitate to one room and avoid another, with no reasoning attached, just a pull or a resistance. That is intuition, intact and unfiltered. Over time, the world imprints on us to distrust that, to explain everything, to only believe what can be proven. The intuitive sense does not disappear. It gets buried under everyone else's certainty.

I think about my own biology teacher, Dr Hope, and an encounter that helped me move through a fear of spiders using nothing but an energetic shift, not logic, not exposure therapy in the clinical sense, something closer to permission. That stayed with me for decades.

What Sensitivity Actually Feels Like

People carry different degrees of energetic sensitivity. Some walk into a home and it feels instantly like home. Others feel drained after twenty minutes in a crowded room for no visible reason. This is not weakness, it is a nervous system picking up more data than most people's, and needing more recovery time to process it.

If this is you, notice your environments on purpose. Which spaces make your shoulders drop. Which people leave you steadier than you arrived, and which ones leave you depleted, regardless of how the conversation went on the surface.

If You Love an Energetically Sensitive Person

Do not force it. Create safety instead of pressure. Let them have distance without reading it as rejection. Ask them what they are sensing rather than arguing them out of it. And know that touch, closeness, sound, all land differently for a sensitive nervous system, sometimes as pleasure, sometimes as too much, often as both in the same five minutes.

"Trust your intuition. Reclaim the awareness you had before the world told you not to."
