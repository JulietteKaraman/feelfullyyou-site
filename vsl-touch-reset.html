The Why

Your body isn't waiting for the right mood. It's bracing.

That's a reflex, trained in by every unequal, unsafe, or unsatisfying experience you've ever had with touch.

A University of Toronto study found this is exactly why up to 55% of women lose desire, and it's developmental, not hormonal. Learned. Which means it can be unlearned.

The Turn

Everyone sells you spontaneity. It won't work.

Spontaneity asks a braced body to leap. A braced body doesn't leap. It waits to see if this is safe.

When spontaneity fails, the next thing everyone reaches for is technique. More moves, more effort, more variety, layered onto a body that isn't responsive yet. That's teaching someone to dance on a leg that's still numb.

I don't sell spark, and I don't sell technique. I teach you to work with what's actually present in your body right now. Just what is. From there, it only gets better.

The Method

Four parts, always in this order.

Know your own wiring first. Then structured conversation. Then structured touch dates, starting with the non-sexual kind. Then the rituals that keep it alive.

Structure, not spontaneity. Safety before pleasure, every time, because a nervous system that's still bracing can't feel much of anything else.

Why Twelve Weeks

Think of it like rebooting a phone that's slowed to a crawl.

Somewhere along the way you were loaded with instructions about touch, about sex, about how a relationship is supposed to work. All of it running in the background. None of it actually yours.

Twelve weeks clears the cache. It empties out what got crammed in and restarts from what's actually true for your body. That becomes the foundation for everything after. With your partner. With yourself. With the people around you. With your children.
