The Why

A brace. A tightening. Checking out. Tracking them instead of feeling yourself.

The Turn

Everyone sells you spontaneity. But what if we've got it backwards?

Spontaneity. Date nights. New techniques. More effort. More variety.

We keep layering more onto the experience before we've stopped to ask what's actually happening in the body.

Are you bracing? Barely feeling anything? Highly sensationful but not actually experiencing pleasure? Tracking them instead of feeling yourself?

I don't sell spark, and I don't sell technique. I teach you to work with what's actually present in your body right now. Just what is. From there, it only gets better.

The Method

Four parts, always in this order.

  1. 1 Recognise: Notice what's actually happening in your body.
  2. 2 Discern: Is this my truth, or is this my pattern?
  3. 3 Discover: What can I feel? What's pleasurable? What do I actually want?
  4. 4 Ask & Receive: Communicate what you want, and stay with yourself while receiving it.

Structure, not spontaneity. The structure is what creates safety.

Because we're not trying to push past what's there.

We're building your capacity to stay present for more: more sensation, more touch, more pleasure, more truth, more receiving.

Why Twelve Weeks

Think of it like rebooting a phone that's slowed to a crawl.

Somewhere along the way, you picked up instructions about touch, sex, pleasure, relationships, what you should want, how you should respond, what it's supposed to look like.

Some are yours. Some aren't, and some may simply be old patterns still running in the background.

Twelve weeks gives us the time and structure to slow it all down, notice what's actually running, and discover what's true for your body now.

That becomes the foundation for everything after. With your partner. With yourself. In how you communicate, ask, receive and connect.
