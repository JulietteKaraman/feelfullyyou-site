What actually happens in relationship coaching, and how do I know if I need it?

Answered by Juliette Karaman, intimacy and touch expert.

The short answer

Relationship coaching is focused, practical work on the patterns between two people, so that talking, touch and wanting each other start to feel safe again. I am Juliette Karaman, an intimacy and touch expert in the UK, and I do relationship coaching through the body and through touch, because the loop a couple keeps running shows up in the body long before anyone finds the words for it.

One couple came to me after twenty-three years without being intimate. They were on the brink of divorce. The first thing I did was take sex off the table: for the first six weeks, you are not allowed. They laughed. “That’s what we’re here for with you.” Three months later they went on a second honeymoon.

That is relationship coaching the way I do it. Take the pressure out, build safety back into the body, and teach two people how to talk and touch again.

What actually happens during relationship coaching?

We find the loop, we take it out of your bodies so you can both see it, and then we give you a structure to practise something new inside.

  • Find the loop. Say its shape out loud, with no blame and no names in it. "You reach, I brace. You pull back, I feel alone." Most people feel something in the chest drop about half an inch the first time they hear it said as a shape.
  • Find where each of you is. Your Touch Pattern, the way your attention moves when touch arrives, and your Pleasure Language, how you are wired to receive it.
  • Settle the body first. Touch Base®, so each nervous system learns what safe feels like again before anything is asked of the other person.
  • Practise inside a container. The Dyad for words, Touch Dates for touch, the Touch Map™ for finding what each body actually likes now.

Better wording on its own rarely moves it. Most of the people I work with have already rehearsed that one sentence in the shower. When there is no container around it, the words arrive and the other body braces as if it were an ambush.

How do I know if I need relationship coaching?

You probably need it when the same loop keeps running, and trying harder inside it has stopped helping. These are the shapes I see most in couples.

  • The date night got booked, the food was gorgeous, and the drive home still felt empty.
  • One true sentence, said kindly, turns into an argument about something else, or into silence.
  • “I'm fine” has become the easiest thing to say. In my words on the podcast, that “I'm fine” is where intimacy goes to die.
  • A hand lands on a back and a body tightens, before anyone has decided anything.
  • You are kind to each other, and you live like roommates.

It is also for couples where it is good and they want more. Wanting more is a reason all of its own.

How does relationship coaching for couples work with Juliette?

Couples relationship coaching with me starts by naming which stage of touch you are in, because the right next step depends on it.

StageWhat it looks likeWhat helps
The QuietTouch has gone flat. Life got loud and you both stopped reaching.Planned time together, such as a date morning, and structured communication.
The BraceA hand lands and a body tightens in the half second before any thought.Safety in the body first. A date night stacked on top of a brace rarely moves it.
The GoneThe roommate phase. Kind, present in the house, away from each other.The deeper work, held over time, so blame and resentment have somewhere to go.

Most couples I meet are in the second or third stage and keep reaching for the first stage's fix. More effort, more outings, more novelty. When that does nothing, look at the stage before you look at each other.

Is online relationship coaching as good as meeting in person?

Yes. I live in England and work with couples online and in person, and online relationship coaching follows the same structure.

You can start in your own kitchen today. One of you makes the other a cup of tea, and the other keeps adjusting it out loud until it is exactly right. A little more milk. Leave the bag in. Stop there. It sounds silly, and it is the same skill you need in bed: “Can we pause there? Can you go to the left a bit?”

What relationship coaching programs does Juliette offer?

For couples there are two main ways to work with me, and they suit different amounts of time.

  • The Beginning. Three months, completely private, built around the two of you, online wherever you live.
  • A couples VIP day. One full day of the work together, which includes the Touch Map.
  • The Intimacy and Communication Cards. The cards that go into the Dyad, for couples who want to start at home.

Picture the two of you a year from now, still running the same loop. Then picture the morning you both hear it as a shape and do something new with it.
