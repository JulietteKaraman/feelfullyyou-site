Remember the beginning?

You could not walk past each other in the kitchen without a hand on a hip. You held hands in the car. A kiss at the door lasted long enough to make you late.

Nobody decided to stop. Touch just got quieter. A peck by the kettle. A pat on the arm. Two people on the same sofa, each on a phone, a foot of cushion between you that neither of you remembers putting there.

You are still in love. You are just out of practice.

Thirty-one days is how you fall back in. Small touches, a lot of laughing, and a body that slowly remembers this person is someone it likes to be near.

Is this you?

You reach for them. They pull away.

A hug at the sink gets a pat on the arm. So you’ve started reaching less.

They reach for you. You freeze.

You love them. Your body still tenses, and you don’t know why.

Nobody reaches anymore.

Great team. Brilliant parents. Somewhere along the way, flatmates.

Whichever one you are, the 31 days start in the same place.

Couples come to me who have not been intimate in nine months. In five years. In twenty-three years.

Almost every one of them wants a five-hour session. Fix it all in one go.

I say no.

Five hours is a mountain, and a body that has learned to brace will brace the whole way up.

So we go the other way. Thirty-one small doses, each one short enough to say yes to. Each one ends on time, while it still feels good, with nothing expected after.

That is the part that changes everything. When your body knows a touch is no down payment on something else, it stops guarding the door and starts to lean in.

Every day you finish is one more piece of proof. By day thirty-one, your body has a whole month of it.

Why a month and not a weekend

Nobody walks into a gym after ten years off and does three hours.

You would pull something and you would never go back. You do twenty minutes, you leave while it still feels good, and you come back tomorrow.

Every day in here has a time on it, and the time is the magic. Three minutes, then done. Nothing follows unless you both want it to.

Stopping while it is still good is what makes you both want tomorrow.

The 31 days

Five weeks. Each one opens a little more.

Days1-7
Play
This is where the fun comes back. A date you actually choose. Ninety seconds at your own front door. A six-second kiss. The two of you holding each other up, back to back, on purpose, probably laughing.
You leave with: the feeling of being silly together again.
Days8-14
Noticing
Three minutes of really looking at each other, and saying stop when something shifts. Finding where your own yes and no live in your body, before anybody touches anybody.
You leave with: a yes you can feel, and a no that costs nothing.
Days15-21
Giving
The first real asks. Touch me as if to make me feel held, five different ways. Touch me without using your hands. The rule that makes it work: do not ask if I liked it.
You leave with: giving that feels like a gift, with no test attached.
Days22-26
Opening
Touch with nowhere to go, getting slower and longer. Halving the pace, then halving it again. Asking out loud for exactly what you want, while it is happening.
You leave with: the words for what you want, said out loud.
Days27-31
Staying
The longest ones of the month. A dance. Bare backs holding each other up. An inner elbow explored as if you had never touched it. One of you doing nothing at all but receive.
You leave with: two people who stay, in the touch and with each other.

Questions

Before you start

What if my partner is not keen?

Start anyway. Show them day one, it takes a few minutes and asks almost nothing. Plenty of the month asks for no physical contact at all. Curiosity tends to do the rest.

Is any of it sexual?

None of it. Every practice fits inside an ordinary day, with the kids asleep upstairs.

How much time does it take?

Most days, under three minutes. The last week has the longer ones, and by then you will want them.

What if we miss a day?

It waits. Tomorrow's practice only opens once you have done today's, so you never fall behind and you never have to catch up.

We have not touched in years. Is it too late?

Couples come to me after twenty-three years. This is the gentlest place there is to start. If you want me with you in person, that is The Beginning.
