Questions

What if my partner isn't keen?

Show them day one. It takes three minutes and asks almost nothing.

Is any of it sexual?

No. Every one fits inside an ordinary day. Fair warning, though: when the small touches come back, the bigger ones tend to follow. With the couples I work with, the bedroom often warms up on its own, and nobody had to push for it.

We're not very touchy-feely people. Is this for us?

Yes. Some days ask for no touch at all, just a look across the room or a few words. The ones that do start small, like a hand on a shoulder. You go at your own pace, and you find the kind of touch that suits the two of you.
