How it works

Fifteen minutes. One card.
The other person may say only three things.

THANK YOU
I understood you.
CLARIFY THAT
I'm listening, but I don't understand yet.
SUMMARISE THAT
Give me the bottom line.
No fixing. No defending. No "that is not what happened." You do not have to agree. You only have to understand.

Isn't putting it on a timer a bit unromantic?
