With a partner

15 minutes. One card. Face to face if you can.

  1. Agree together: for the next 15 minutes, our only aim is to understand each other. We stay with one prompt. We won't interject or fix. We don't have to like or agree with what the other person says.
  2. One of you reads the card. The other takes it in, breathes, and gives one clear communication.
  3. The listener answers with only one of three responses:
Thank youI understood you.
Clarify thatI'm listening, but I don't understand yet. Add the missing piece.
Summarise thatThere's too much for me to take in. Take a breath and give me the bottom line.

The aim is always to get to "Thank you": to be understood. You don't have to like what was said, or even agree with it.

Once you reach "Thank you", switch roles and use the same prompt again. Keep going back and forth until the timer ends. Each round goes a little deeper.

Afterwards, let it settle. No picking apart what was said.

On your own

Journal it, or speak it out loud. Both work.

  1. Pull a card and set the timer for 10 minutes.
  2. Write one answer, or speak one answer out loud. Just one.
  3. Take a breath. Read the question again, or say it out loud.
  4. Write or speak your next answer. Excavate one answer at a time.
  5. Keep returning to the same prompt until the timer ends. First answers are the surface. Stay with it.
  6. When the timer ends, pause and notice how your body feels. Often there's a little more breath, more space, more aliveness.
