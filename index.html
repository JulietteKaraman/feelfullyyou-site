My method

The Touch Reset

Being touched and actually feeling touch are two different things.

Before we try to fix your sex life, bring back the spark or find another technique, I want to know: What is actually happening in your body when touch and sensation arrive?

That question became my method. I call it The Touch Reset. First, we find two things:

Your Touch Pattern

What you tend to do when you're in connection.

Holder · Operator · Wanderer
Your Pleasure Language

What kind of touch and sensation is your way into pleasure.

Energetic · Sensual · Sexual · Kinky · Shapeshifter

The Touch Reset

Then we give your body something to hold on to.

A body that's been through something can't open with nothing to hold on to. So every practice has edges.A start. An end. An anchor. A few words.

01

Touch Base®

Thumb to forefinger. Feel the ridges. "I am safe right now." Two minutes. The anchor that tells your body it's safe again, wherever you are.

02

Touch Dates

Timed touch. You start at three minutes and build to fifteen, then thirty. Your body always knows when it ends, so it can stay.

03

The Dyad

Timed talk. One prompt from the Cards, held for fifteen minutes, and only three answers: "Thank you." "Clarify that." "Summarise that." So the hard things get said.

04

The Touch Map™

Your whole body, mapped. Every part rated green, orange or red, so you know exactly how you're wired, and how to ask for it.

From there, you learn to notice what's actually happening in your body, discover what you want, and communicate it.

Your body becomes a compass you know how to read.

The Cards, in my own life

I couldn't write my sister's eulogy.

When my sister died, I was meant to write her eulogy. I couldn't.

So I walked through Amsterdam on the phone with my coach, and she asked me the same thing, again and again. What else? What did you hold back? What do you need to tell her?

Underneath the shock of her dying, the grief and the pain of losing her, there was fury. Fury, not at her, but at the situation. The things we always talked about doing together: our sister trips, skiing, our kids' weddings, becoming grandmothers together, all of it.

Underneath the fury was acceptance. That future we'd pictured was never going to come.

Fifteen minutes on one prompt got me there. I call it clearing the pipes.

I used Touch Base and the Cards with my mother as she was dying. I use them with every client. It's how people go from feeling nothing to feeling again.
