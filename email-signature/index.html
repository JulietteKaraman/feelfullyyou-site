Your Gmail signature, every part clicks through

  1. Drag to select the whole banner inside the dotted box, then press Cmd + C.
  2. In Gmail: cog (top right) → See all settings → General tab.
  3. Scroll to Signature → Create new → name it "Juliette" → click in the box → Cmd + V.
  4. Under "Signature defaults", choose it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the very bottom → Save Changes. Then send yourself a test email and click each part.

On every option: the photo, name, text and green band go to feelfullyyou.com, the phone number dials you, the email opens a message to you, and the round icons open Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and the podcast.
