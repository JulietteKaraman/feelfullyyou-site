What's inside

Connection does not break. It goes quiet.

Two people who love each other, finding the patterns that close the door between them, and learning how to open it again. One conversation avoided. One thing left unsaid. I have worked with hundreds of couples inside exactly this. The love is still there. What is gone is the language to reach each other. The words that feel honest rather than rehearsed. The prompt that opens something instead of starting another circular conversation.
