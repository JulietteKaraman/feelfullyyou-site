# Where Did the Touch Go? Book outline, draft 1

19 Nov 2026. Working title. Paid book at £17 (price lives on the sales page only, never in the book).
About 22,000 words. Written from Juliette's existing essays and podcast, not from scratch.
Status: OUTLINE ONLY. No chapter has been written. Chapter sources below were chosen from essay titles and opening paragraphs; the full essays have not all been read yet.

## Job of the book

The quiz tells her where her Touch went. The book lets her feel it, gives her one small thing to do, and ends at Scrumptious Confessions (free fortnight). It must also prove Juliette can help, by actually helping.

Voice rules for every chapter: start in the moment, not the lesson. Short lines. No fixing energy. No em dashes, no sentences starting with "And", no "Not x but y", never the word "broken", nervous system language only rarely, Touch always capital T. Each chapter carries at least one real result (see "Proof" below).

## Proof to place (each chapter needs at least one)

From the voice skill's reference of client results. Every one must be confirmed by Juliette and be anonymous or have consent before it goes in print:
- A couple who had not had sex in 23 years reached a second honeymoon in three months.
- A woman who wanted to leave her husband realised after a month that she had been pointing her own shame at him.
- Touch Base as a one-touch anchor for fear of flying that still works years later.
- A woman who could not receive attention without redirecting it, and now directs it.

Numbers (750 women, 600 couples, clients in 20 countries) are not used until Juliette confirms they are current.

## One door, no menu

The last page points only to the Confessions free fortnight. The Touch Reset call is never mentioned in the book. It is offered later, by email, to people who are ready. Touch Reset pricing never appears.
