What is happening

The dynamic is off. The safety is uncertain.

You still care. Somewhere along the way, reaching became something you prepared for instead of something that just happened.

He reaches and she stiffens slightly. Just enough.

She waits for him to notice and pull back. He does. The distance closes in small degrees until it feels like it has always been there. Both of you still want the same thing. You've just lost the felt sense of how to get there together.

You were both told The Spontaneity Lie: that real closeness arrives on its own, and building it on purpose isn't the real thing. So you waited for it to come back. The distance grew in the waiting.

I'm an EMDR therapist, a hypnotherapist and a coach, so you get the best of all of it. You won't spend the day talking about things. We take it to the body.
