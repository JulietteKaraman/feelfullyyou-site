How the 12 weeks work

Four parts. One body, yours.

01

Recognise

What is my body already doing?

You'll learn how to:

  • Notice what your body does around touch: the bracing, the performing, the pulling away

  • Slow down enough to feel what is actually happening
02

Is this my truth, or my pattern?

You'll learn how to:

What actually feels good to me?

Before you decide

Questions you might have.

Do I need a partner?

No. It begins with your relationship with your own body. If you have a partner, they don't need to join. There are practices you can choose to explore together, and their taking part is never required.

What if I have a history of trauma?

I have been working with trauma and the body for the last 10 years, and I experience it deeply myself. This is designed to meet you where you are. You will never be asked to push through a no, override your body's response or share anything you don't want to share. You choose your pace, your boundaries and what you take part in. It's a group programme, and it doesn't replace individual therapy or medical care.

What if I'm nervous about being seen?

Most women are, at the start. Camera on or off, your choice, and you choose how much you share. The group goes gently, a little more each week.

"In the beginning I didn't want to be visible at all, but then it got easier and easier due to being supported throughout… the pace of the exercises were good because they gradually asked a little more of us each time."Denise Stewart · 30-day women's group

"I am now OK in group situations and don't think others are out to get me."Claire B

How much time will it take?

About two hours a week at most, the live call included. Every call is recorded, so you can always catch up.

How much is it?

There are a few ways to pay, and we go through them on your Touch Reset Call, once we both know this is right for you.

Before then, ask yourself: what would it be worth to you to feel turned on in your own body again, for good?
