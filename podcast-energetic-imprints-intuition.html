Energetic Imprints and the Intuition You Were Taught to Ignore

Everything is made of matter and energy. Here is how to read the imprints around you again, and why most of us stopped.

Juliette KaramanTouch and Intimacy Expert
A solo teaching from The Scrumptious Woman podcast

Everything is made of matter and energy, and we have an innate ability to sense that energy in our surroundings, in the people we sit next to, in a house before we have even seen the second room. As a child, that sense is wide open. Then societal imprints, being told we are imagining things, being told to be logical, being told to explain ourselves, gradually shut it down. The disconnect grows between what we feel and what we are permitted to say we feel.

Where My Own Sense Came Back Online

I think often about my biology teacher, Dr Hope, and how an encounter with her helped me move through a long-held fear of spiders, not through logic or exposure, but through something closer to an energetic release. That single experience taught me the intuitive channel had never actually closed. It had only gone quiet under everything I had been told to prioritise instead.

Energetic Blueprints and Pleasure

Our energetic makeup shapes more than intuition, it shapes pleasure, self-awareness, and how we bond. As an erotic blueprint coach, I see this constantly: two people can receive the exact same touch and have an entirely different experience of it, because their energetic wiring reads contact differently. Neither is wrong. Knowing your own blueprint changes how you ask to be touched, and how you touch someone else.

Navigating the World With This Kind of Sensitivity

If you are energetically sensitive, sensory overload is real and it is not a character flaw. Build in grounding, time in nature, touch that you have chosen, music that regulates rather than overstimulates you. If you love someone who is energetically sensitive, create the safe space rather than requiring them to explain themselves out of it. Respect their need for distance. Let closeness happen on their terms.

"Honour your intuitive senses. Authentically embrace your unique energy, rather than editing it down to be believed."
