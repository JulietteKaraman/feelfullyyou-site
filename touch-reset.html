My method

The Touch Reset

Being touched and actually feeling touch are two different things.

Juliette KaramanIntimacy and touch expert

Before we try to fix your sex life, bring back the spark or find another technique, I want to know one thing: what is actually happening in your body when touch and sensation arrive?

That question became my method. I call it The Touch Reset. It is everything I teach, in two movements. First, we find out how you are wired. Then we give your body structure it can hold on to.

First, we find three things

  • Your Touch Pattern: Holder, Operator or Wanderer. What you tend to do when you're in connection.
  • Your Pleasure Language: Energetic, Sensual, Sexual, Kinky or Shapeshifter. What kind of touch and sensation is your way into pleasure. These come from Jaiya's Erotic Blueprint™ framework. I'm a certified Erotic Blueprint coach, and Jaiya is one of my mentors.
  • Your Desire Style: Responsive or Spontaneous. Whether desire arrives first, or once touch has begun.

For a lot of couples, this alone changes things. Once both of you can see where your wiring differs, the reaching adjusts on its own. For others it is the foundation, and the structure below is where the work happens.

Then, safety first: structure your body can hold on to

Safety comes before anything else. A body that has been through something can't open with nothing to hold on to. That is why I build the container first: three structures, each with edges. A start. An end. A few words. Touch Base® is taught alongside them, as the anchor to come back to.

01 · Timed, structured communication: the Dyad

One prompt from the Cards. Fifteen minutes. Three answers only: "Thank you." "Clarify that." "Summarise that." No fixing, no defending, no advice.

The speaker stops managing what they say. The listener stops interpreting what they hear. Things that have been circling for months or years start to land, and the body opens to touch the same way it opens to being heard.

Read more about the Dyad →

02 · Timed, non-sexual touch: Touch Dates

Touch with nothing required at the end. You start at three minutes and build up to fifteen, then thirty. This is capacity building: the body learns to stay as the time grows.

Week one might be hands. Week two, arms and shoulders. The range grows because your body, having felt the structure hold several times, decides it is safe to go further. That decision can't be forced. It can only be made possible.

03 · The time limit

The timer ends it, so nobody has to decide they've had enough and the mood doesn't have to carry the ending. Knowing exactly when it stops is what lets the body stay.

The anchor: Touch Base®

Thumb to forefinger. Feel the ridges. "I am safe right now." Hold for two minutes. The anchor that tells your body it's safe again, wherever you are.

Later: the Touch Map

Once the body trusts the container, we map it. Your whole body, asked part by part. Green, yes. Orange, maybe. Red, stop. You learn to steer touch until it lands, and your partner learns to read you.

"Intimacy comes back with structure. The Touch Reset is the structure."

Why I work with each partner alone first

Most couples work puts two people in the same room and asks them to talk through what is between them. That assumes both bodies can stay regulated enough to be present. In practice, the real work happens in the body, alone, before the conversation can land.

Watch what runs when two activated bodies sit on the same sofa. She begins to speak. Within ninety seconds his shoulders rise, his jaw sets, his breath climbs, and he is somewhere decades old. Her body registers his tightness. Her chest closes. She speaks faster because she can feel him going. He goes anyway. Two stress responses, feeding each other.

So I work with each partner on their own first. Each one builds capacity in the body: to feel a sensation without being overrun by it, and to find where a reaction actually comes from. Somatic Experiencing and EMDR give a route into the charge that has been firing in the marriage for years.

When the couple comes back together, the conversation that felt impossible for years can take about twenty minutes, because it has already happened in the bodies. This is the structure behind The Beginning.
