# Title, subtitle and forewords

*Draft 1, 21 Nov 2026. This is front matter, not a chapter. Review notes are at the bottom of this file and are not part of the book.*

---

## Working title

**Where Did the Touch Go?**

## Subtitle options

1. Why you stopped feeling it, and the small, structured way back
2. A 31-day guide back to feeling, wanting and being touched
3. Find where your Touch went, why, and how to bring it back
4. Why Touch stopped landing, and what to do about it in 31 days

## Forewords

Juliette is going to ask three coaches to write a foreword: Naomi Cambridge, Suean McMara and Christine Borsnick (spellings to be confirmed). Each one is 300 to 400 words, and they run one after another, with the author's name and a line about who they are. Three is the most. Any other kind words go on the cover and the sales page as short quotes.
