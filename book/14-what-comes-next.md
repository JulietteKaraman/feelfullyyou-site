*Draft 1, 21 Nov 2026. Built from Canon, the Confessions launch thread and the Scrumptious Touch Reset page. No prices. Review notes are at the bottom of this file and are not part of the book.*

You have the book, and you have a plan. Here is everything that comes afterwards, in the order I would take it.

## First, keep going

Thirty-one days of Touch Base. Then thirty more. It is the cheapest, quickest and most reliable thing in this book.

## Then, come inside

Scrumptious Confessions is where all of this keeps happening, with company.

Every Monday, a confession in my voice. Touch Base every day, with a tracker. A card on Tuesdays and Saturdays, a poem on Wednesdays, and your questions answered every Thursday, with no name on them. A meditation on Fridays, and a new place on your Touch Map each month you stay.

It is open to women and men. The first seven days are free, and the Holiday Survival Kit is yours in that week, whatever you decide.

> **[Design note, not book text]** QR code and link here to Scrumptious Confessions. File: `book/assets/qr-scrumptious-confessions.png` (opens feelfullyyou.com/scrumptious-confessions).

## If you are a woman, and you want the whole of this held

The Scrumptious Touch Reset is for women. Recorded modules, with live support. It runs in four stages: Recognise, Discern, Discover, Ask and Receive. You have already taken the first steps of the first.

I open it in January. If you want to know whether it is right for you, I will invite you to a Touch Reset call.

## If you are doing this together

The Beginning is for couples. A VIP day is a half day, in person or online, built around the two of you.

Men, you are welcome in Confessions, and the couples route is open to you.

## Why I leave this behind

People ask me why I have a podcast. Why I write on Substack. Why it matters so much to me to help.

I think about my deathbed. I picture myself lying there, asking one question. If what I have done has helped just one person, is that enough?

I have decided that it is. That is the ripple effect. One person feels a little more able to be touched. She holds her daughter differently, or her partner, and it goes on from there, further than I will ever see.

I have asked myself whether it is ego. Here is what I notice instead. It is the message that arrives on Instagram, or Facebook, or in my inbox, from someone I have never met. *Your episode on this taught me exactly what to do, right there, and I have seen such a change.* It is the man who wrote to say that one prompt I gave away for free has completely changed how he and his wife talk to each other.

If I lie there at the end and think, I gave people a little bit of light, then it was worth it.

My nickname is Biem. When I was about two, and people asked what I was called, that is what I said. My name is Juliette Caroline, and in Dutch and in French, Caroline sounds like *biem*. In English, that is beam: a beam of light, a ray of light.

I had to laugh. The universe is not subtle.

## One last thing

I cannot make you do the work. What I can do is help with the questions that come up when you do it.

Everything in this book, I learned in my own body first.

So here is the question one more time. Are you being touched, or are you feeling it? Start with three minutes, and you will know.

All of this is sacred. All of this is welcome.

Warmly,

Juliette
